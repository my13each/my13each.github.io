# 13each

Independent app studio based in Tokyo. This site hosts product information and
privacy policies for the apps we publish.

Studio site: [13each.com](https://13each.com)

---

## Apps

### HushHours

Blocks notifications from the apps you choose, during the hours you choose.
No internet permission — notification content never leaves the device.

- [Privacy policy](./hushhours/) (English · 日本語 · 한국어)

### Battery Charm

Decorates the status bar battery icon with jewelry-style charms.
No account, no analytics — the Accessibility API is used only to find where the battery icon is.

- [Privacy policy](./batterycharm/) (English · 日本語 · 한국어)

### Edge Maestro

One-handed control for your phone through edge gestures.

- [Privacy policy](https://my13each.github.io/EdgeMaestro_Page/) (English · 日本語 · 한국어)
